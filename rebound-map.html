How this is built: a mixture model tuned to three findings from optical shot-tracking research — missed shots carom to the weak side most often, and that bias is strongest on corner and baseline shots but nearly symmetric from the top of the key; longer shots throw longer rebounds (threes reach the free-throw-line area about twice as often as twos); and offensive-rebound rate is U-shaped with shot distance. It's an estimate of tendencies for a single generic miss — not a lookup of any one team's data, and any individual bounce is chaos. Both three-point lines are drawn for reference: the solid teal NBA arc (23′9″, 22′ corners) and the gold dashed NFHS high-school arc (a uniform 19′9″ with no flat corners). The rebound model itself keys off the true distance from the rim, so it works the same at either level. Findings drawn from SportVU / PBPStats rebound-tracking work (Sloan "Deconstructing the Rebound," kenpom, Grantland, Nylon Calculus).
