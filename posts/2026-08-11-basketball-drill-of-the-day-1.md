---
**Setup:**

2 teams of 5 setup on baseline or opposite sidelines.

Players desigate themselves #1 through #5.

**Rules:**

Players #1 for each team play 1v1

If offensive(black in the image) player scores that team moves up to 2v2 and retains the ball (no checking up, ball is live when it clears the 3-point line.

 If the black team (which is now playing 2v2) turns the ball over,  it quickly turns back to 1v1 with the purple team now in possession. 

If purple scores, they are now playing 2v2 when on offense just like black.  

Next team to score will go to 3v3 on offense .

**Winning condition:**  

Team that scores a bucket in 1v1, 2v2,3v3,4v4 and 5v5 wins.

**Why do this drill:**

The speed and chaos created by the tranisitions between offense and defense forces communication, and quick help rotations.  Players get to work on individual 1v1 skill, but also team concepts.
